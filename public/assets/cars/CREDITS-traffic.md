# Traffic vehicle credits

All traffic models in this folder (`traffic_*.glb`) are third-party 3D models licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0).
They were originally published on Sketchfab and fetched from the public Objaverse mirror
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse), which redistributes the Sketchfab files with
their license metadata. Each license was re-checked against the live Sketchfab API on 2026-10-17.

**Changes made (all models):** re-oriented to +Z forward / +Y up, rescaled to real-world meters,
wheels split into separate pivot nodes where possible, materials split/renamed for paint and lamp roles,
geometry welded/decimated, textures resized and re-encoded as WebP, meshopt-compressed.
The original authors do not endorse this project.

**Trademarks:** some models depict real vehicles (Ford Crown Victoria, Fiat Ducato, BMW 3 Series E90,
BMW X5 M) including maker badges. The CC BY license covers the 3D artwork only, not trademark or
trade-dress rights in the vehicle designs, names or logos.
